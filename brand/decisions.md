# Decisions

One line per choice, dated, with why and where to look. Newest at the bottom.

- **2026-11-07** — The library covers the whole brand, not only the app — Feno's PDFs, decks, messages and a future website should look like one thing — see `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-feno-brand-library-design.md`.
- **2026-11-07** — It lives in `brand/` in this repository and splits out later — one maintainer, one codebase; tokens stay in sync with the app and CI checks them — see `README.md` → Splitting out.
- **2026-11-07** — Everything about the look is open except the name, Feno — Belinda wants a stand-out look, not polish on the old one.
- **2026-11-07** — Personality: a trusted field notebook, with consumer-grade finish ("a field notebook with Headspace polish"); greens and earthy colours stay — Belinda's choice from four personalities; references Headspace, Airbnb, Spotify, Slack, Uber, Dribbble — see `references/001`.
- **2026-11-07** — Code is the master, Figma is the mirror — every value stays in git and under CI; Figma is for exploring and looking.
- **2026-11-07** — The first re-skin ships before 1 December, last deploy day Thursday 26 November; anything short waits behind a switch — the in-season rules (README → In season).
- **2026-11-07** — Reference 001 (smart-home dashboard) and four standing sources recorded — see `references/001`, `references/sources.md`.
- **2026-11-07** — Kind colours on their tints are icon pairs, held to 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11), not 4.5 — the token test showed orange at 4.22 in light; the hue sits on the icon and the edge, never on text — see `tokens.json` → `$extensions.feno.pairs`, `src/lib/tokens.test.ts`.
- **2026-11-07** — Three visual directions drawn and published for Belinda to choose between (A Paper and leaf, B Orchard at dusk, C Field notebook) — the personality is fixed, so the choice is palette, type and shape; the kind colours are the same in all three so they do not sway it — see `references/002-directions/note.md`.
- **2026-11-07** — Direction **B, Orchard at dusk**, chosen by Belinda: cool soil greys, bright leaf green, fig purple and pomegranate red as the two accents, Bricolage Grotesque for display and Source Sans 3 for body, crisp 14 px corners, 1 px lines, no shadows — the most confident and data-forward of the three — see `references/002-directions/`.
- **2026-11-07** — Feno serves its own fonts (Bricolage Grotesque and Source Sans 3, latin subsets, SIL OFL) from `public/fonts`; Google Fonts leaves the content security policy, the service worker and the privacy policy, which becomes version 20 dated for the release — one fewer third party, the app fully offline, first paint no longer waits on a font server — see `tokens.json` → `$extensions.feno.fonts`, `scripts/fonts.test.mjs`.
- **2026-11-08** — Three marks and three wordmarks drawn for the choice (leaf and sun redrawn; fig and pomegranate; an F of leaves; Feno plain; Feno with a leaf in the o; FENO spaced) — see `logo/explorations/`.
- **2026-11-08** — Logo: **mark 1** (the leaf and sun, redrawn on the leaf plate) with **wordmark 2** (Feno in Bricolage Grotesque 800, a leaf in the o), chosen by Belinda — the mark growers know, made crisp; the wordmark carries the one flourish — see `logo.md`, `logo/`.
- **2026-11-08** — Figma file "Feno brand" (key XNIou2IncdVbQeYsjAYjZt): three pages on the starter plan, the variable collections loaded from `figma-variables.json` as Primitives, Theme Light, Theme Dark and Scale (one mode per collection on this plan), the core pieces bound to them on *Components*; code stays the master — see `README.md` → Figma.
- **2026-11-08** — Depth, from reference 003: cards, tiles and stat tiles rest on the page with a two-layer shadow (`--elevation`), the icon sits on a round plate, and the mark's leaf is the wallpaper, large and faint, top right of every screen — Belinda found the flat screens one-dimensional; direction B's "no shadows" gives way to this — see `references/003-leaf-landing`, `styles.css` → Depth.
- **2026-11-08** — The dark theme's ground is reference 004's deep greens (#051F20 background, #0B2B26 surface, #163832 second surface, #235347 soft line, #8EB69B muted), and the light tint is its mint #DAF1DE — every text pair still clears 4.5:1; #8EB69B is not the action colour because it is 2.25:1 on white — see `references/004-green-ramp`.
- **2026-11-08** — The wordmark replaces the typed "Feno" in the header, in the ink of the theme, with the leaf in the o — see `logo.md`.
- **2026-11-08** — Imagery: shaded SVG, not rendered images — the icons and the leaf keep their shapes and gain light, shadow and a soft inner edge drawn in; the growth-stage pictures become clay-like (matte, rounded, lit from above) so a grower relates to them — Belinda's choice from reference 005; rendered images would cost a megabyte per phone and could not take the theme — see `references/005-plant-renders`, `illustration.md`.
- **2026-11-08** — Released on Sunday 8 November, before the in-season window (PR #324, merged as 781db41); privacy policy version 20 dated the same day.
- **2026-11-08** — The imagery built as decided: all 21 stage drawings clay-like (one filter, a gradient per plant colour, a shadow onto a plate in the new `stage-plate` tokens, so the plate no longer glares in dark mode), and the icons' `duo` fill lit from above (42 to 12 percent) while the line stays flat — Belinda approved the prototype of six stages for all 21 and the icon fills; the mark and the wallpaper stay flat so they print and scale — see `illustration.md`, `src/components/StageArt.tsx`, `src/components/Icon.tsx`.
- **2026-11-08** — From the first grower feedback on the new look: edit and delete are icon buttons (the header's round `iconbtn`, a red variant for delete), named for a screen reader and on hover, in a card's top right corner or in a row; a card's full-width buttons stay for what growers do every day. The Log button on a block card is the floating bar's Log, a filled pill. The 7-day forecast on Home is a bordered row with a chevron, not a coloured heading: growers missed that it opened. Delete my account is one quiet line at the foot of the profile; the confirm screen carries the explanation. On a desktop the Timeline's filters stack in their column instead of running over the cards — see `spacing.md` → Targets, `src/styles.css`.
