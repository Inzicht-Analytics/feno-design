# Identity

## The name

**Feno.** Always written with a capital F and lower-case letters, in running text and in the wordmark. Never FENO, never "the Feno app". From *fenologie*, phenology: the study of when a plant does what. The name is the one thing about the brand that is fixed (`decisions.md`, 7 November 2026).

## What Feno is

A season diary for fig and pomegranate growers in the Western Cape, in English and Afrikaans, that works in the orchard without signal. It records growth stages, sprays, fertiliser, irrigation, pruning, pest counts, pollination, harvests and lab results, shows the weather and the heat units, forecasts the harvest, and lets a grower share what they choose with their exporter. It is free to use. It never sells or hands out a grower's data.

## The promise

**Your season, kept.** Everything that happened on the farm this season, in one place, written the way a grower would say it, there when the signal is not.

## Personality

A trusted field notebook with consumer-grade finish. In three words: **calm, crisp, knowing.**

- **Calm.** Nothing shouts. One thing matters on a screen and it is the largest thing there. Motion is for feedback, never decoration.
- **Crisp.** Lines are one pixel, corners are moderate, type is confident. Cards rest on the page with a soft shadow, so they read as objects (`references/003`).
- **Knowing.** Feno knows orchards. The words are a grower's words. The pictures are drawn, not stock. The green is a leaf, the pink is a pomegranate.

## What Feno is not

- Not a dashboard. The grower comes first, the exporter second, the chart third.
- Not an adviser. Feno shows what happened and what the forecast says. It never recommends a spray or a dose.
- Not playful. No mascots, no confetti, no exclamation marks.
- Not corporate. No stock photos of handshakes, no gradients for their own sake.

## Who it speaks to

| Person | Where they are | What the brand must do for them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The grower | In the orchard, in sun, with gloves, on a phone, often in Afrikaans | Be readable at arm's length and tappable with a thumb. Be quick. Never lose an entry. |
| The farm manager or worker | The same, with less to decide | Show the one thing to do next. |
| The exporter's technical staff | At a desk, on a laptop, with ten farms open | Be trustworthy and precise. Show the season at a glance. |
| Belinda, Feno's maker | Everywhere | Carry the same look into a PDF, a deck, a message and a website without a designer on call. |

## The one line

When the brand needs a sentence: *Feno is the season diary for fig and pomegranate growers: your whole season, in your words, with or without signal.*

In Afrikaans: *Feno is die seisoendagboek vir vye- en granaatboere: jou hele seisoen, in jou eie woorde, met of sonder sein.*
