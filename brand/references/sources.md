# Standing sources

Galleries and libraries worth searching when a piece needs a reference. A specific shot that shapes a decision becomes a numbered folder beside this file.

- **Muzli, "Top 25 app UI design inspiration ideas #10"** — https://medium.muz.li/top-25-app-ui-design-inspiration-ideas-10-1c321cafb141 — curated shots; good for headline and card treatments, empty states.
- **Dribbble, popular mobile** — https://dribbble.com/shots/popular/mobile — concept work, strong on finish and motion; search "farm", "weather", "journal", "harvest".
- **Refero** — https://refero.design/ — real shipped screens by flow; search "onboarding", "empty state", "settings", "offline".
- **Mobbin, latest iOS apps** — https://mobbin.com/discover/apps/ios/latest — whole flows of real apps; look at Headspace (warmth and motion), Airbnb (type and space), Spotify (dark mode), Slack and Uber (clarity).
